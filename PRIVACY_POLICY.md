# Privacy Policy for Tide Buoy

Effective Date: March 16, 2026

Tide Buoy ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data may be collected when you use the Tide Buoy app and how it is used.

## Data We May Process

- Precise location (with permission) to find nearby tide stations and buoy data.
- Purchase status for subscriptions and tips via Apple StoreKit.
- Limited diagnostics and performance data to keep the app stable.
- Ad-related identifiers and interaction data through Google AdMob in the free tier.

We do not require account creation.

## How Data Is Used

- App functionality (tides, buoy readings, map spot selection).
- Subscription and purchase handling.
- Ad delivery in the free version.
- Diagnostics and reliability improvements.

## Tracking and Advertising

Tide Buoy uses Google AdMob for advertising. Ad-related data handling is governed by Google and Apple platform policies.

## Third Parties

The app may communicate with:

- Apple services (StoreKit and platform services).
- Google AdMob.
- NOAA public data endpoints for tide and buoy information.

## Your Choices

- You can deny location permission in iOS Settings.
- You can manage subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact: support@tidebuoy.app
